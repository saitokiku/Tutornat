My recommendation is **Room to Reason**.

**Make room for understanding.**

It gives Manny an education company name with a clear belief behind it: children deserve time, good explanations, and room to think. It can carry an AI tutor today and reading, human tutoring, camps, and schools later.

**This is a creative recommendation pending clearance.** I completed the brand work and a preliminary web screen. Live USPTO database searches and authoritative domain checks were blocked in this read-only environment. I have not represented those checks as completed or called any name available.

I followed the installed kit’s context → strategy → naming → identity → voice method, supplemented by its messaging and architecture skills.

**1. Positioning: useful help now, understanding that can be demonstrated later.**

The primary buyer is a US parent whose child gets stuck on homework, particularly as arithmetic becomes algebra. The wider audience is approximately 8–17; the initial offer must describe the actual mathematics covered rather than imply a complete curriculum for every age.

The parent wants relief without wondering whether the software simply completed the assignment. The child wants an explanation that makes sense, without embarrassment or another lecture.

Room to Reason replaces the nightly scramble between a frustrated parent, scattered videos, answer tools, and tutoring appointments. It can also complement a human tutor.

The positioning statement:

> For families facing difficult homework nights, Room to Reason provides AI tutoring that starts with the assignment, teaches the missing foundations, and reports separately what happened with help and what the child demonstrated independently.

The intended distinction is the **complete Homework → Repair → Prove journey**, especially the honesty of the record. “We guide instead of giving answers” is insufficient differentiation: Khanmigo already makes that promise. IXL already offers diagnostic insights and personalized next steps. Our execution must earn the distinction. [Khanmigo](https://www.khanacademy.org/khan-labs), [IXL Diagnostic](https://www.ixl.com/benchmark/info).

The brand commits to four things:

| Principle | What families should experience |
|---|---|
| **Care** | Questions and mistakes receive patient, respectful responses. |
| **Understanding** | The explanation reaches the idea behind the homework step. |
| **Honesty** | Practice, independent checks, missing evidence, and next steps remain distinguishable. |
| **Access** | The $49 offer has clear coverage and usage limits before purchase. |

It refuses to promise guaranteed grades, instant mastery, human-tutor equivalence, perfect AI accuracy, unlimited use, or a staffed crisis response. An independent check also cannot establish that no outside person or device helped.

Manny’s deeper mission should become a public commitment: **help young people develop knowledge and judgment they can use throughout life.** Parents should hear about that purpose alongside homework help from the first page.

**2. Names: twelve candidates across four distinct directions.**

The naming brief is **meaningful, warm, credible in a parent recommendation, acceptable to a teenager, and broad enough for a lifetime education business**. These rankings are editorial judgment, not consumer testing.

The four directions are:

- **Learning words:** established concepts with substantive meaning.
- **Plain service names:** immediately understandable and trustworthy.
- **Invitations and expressions:** a belief conveyed through ordinary language.
- **Material metaphors:** learning as something connected, cultivated, or made carefully.

**3. Voice: calm clarity for parents; patient, specific encouragement for children.**

To a parent, Room to Reason sounds like an educator who can explain what happened and what comes next. Lead with the useful finding, distinguish observation from inference, and make uncertainty understandable.

To a child, it gives one manageable next step. Praise a specific action—“You checked both sides”—rather than intelligence, speed, or obedience. An eight-year-old gets simpler language; a seventeen-year-old gets equal warmth without baby talk.

Use **try, explain, notice, practice, check, with help, independently, next**. Avoid **genius, behind, effortless, guaranteed, revolutionary, mastery unlocked**. “Repair” can remain internal terminology; children should hear “Let’s look at the step underneath this.”

These rewrites use actual inherited product surfaces. The new copy describes intended launch behavior; the report examples are illustrative.

| Surface | Existing copy | Proposed parent copy | Proposed child copy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Signup** | “Create an account,” followed by a free-minutes offer. | **“Start with tonight’s math. $49/month. See covered topics and usage limits before you join.”** | After parent setup: **“What are you working on? I’m an AI tutor. You can ask me to explain a step another way.”** |
| **Weekly report** | “Nothing new was confirmed this week.” | **“Sam worked on fractions with help this week. One independent check passed; the follow-up is still due.”** | **“You solved a new fraction problem without hints. We’ll check it again later to see what stuck.”** |
| **Crisis referral** | A long paragraph beginning “I’m really glad you told me…” | After a recorded referral: **“A message raised a safety concern, and the lesson stopped. Crisis resources were shown. Please check in with your child now.”** | **“Let’s pause the lesson. You deserve support from a person.”** Follow immediately with the actions below. |

The child’s **self-harm referral screen** should continue:

> **If you are hurt or in immediate danger, call 911 now.**  
> In the US, call or text **988** for help with thoughts of suicide or hurting yourself. You can ask an adult you trust to help you make the call.  
>   
> I’m an AI tutor. I can’t contact emergency services for you.

Keep the resources visible when tutoring stops. Keep a separate abuse referral that identifies a safe adult without presuming the account holder is safe. Never imply that showing this screen has connected the child to a responder. The distinction between 988 support and immediate-danger emergency response follows [SAMHSA guidance](https://www.samhsa.gov/sites/default/files/fact-sheet-key-differences-988-vs-911.pdf).

**4. Identity: the warmth of a good book, with the clarity of a useful learning tool.**

The identity should feel carefully made and approachable at $49. It should hold up on a parent’s report, a teenager’s screen, and a future camp sign.

| Color | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Mulberry ink** | `#4C3048` | Wordmark, headings, primary buttons |
| **Warm paper** | `#FAF6ED` | Main background |
| **Graphite** | `#272A28` | Body text and mathematical work |
| **Ochre** | `#E5B654` | Small highlights and moments of discovery |
| **Soft leaf** | `#DCE5D8` | Quiet supporting panels |

Use paper and dark text generously. Ochre is an accent, not small text on white. Progress states need words and symbols as well as color; success should not depend on identifying a green dot.

**Type:** Source Serif 4 for the wordmark direction and major headings; Source Sans 3 for explanations, controls, reports, and small labels. The serif gives the company an editorial character; the sans keeps the product practical. Both are open-source Adobe families, designed to complement one another. [Source Serif](https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-serif), [Source Sans](https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-sans).

**Logo idea:** begin with a custom wordmark, not a mascot. Give the capital **R** a broad bowl and a firmly planted leg; use generous spacing and open counters. A stacked “Room / to Reason” arrangement can work on narrow surfaces. Develop any small symbol from the lettering after the wordmark works in one color.

**Images:** authentic work in progress—an erased attempt, a diagram, a question in the margin. Use licensed or consented photography of varied ages and ordinary homes. Do not manufacture testimonials or imply that pictured adults are included live tutors.

**Never:** glowing brains, robots, sparkles, neon gradients, coding brackets, terminal typography, rocket badges, cartoon “geniuses,” or invented school crests. Avoid making “affordable” look disposable or “trustworthy” look like borrowed accreditation.

Use one company identity:

- **Room to Reason Math** — initial offering.
- **Room to Reason Reading** — later subject expansion.
- **Room to Reason Tutors** — only for an actual staffed service.
- **Room to Reason Camps** — later physical programs.

The interface continues to label the software **AI tutor**. The company carries the warm name.

**5. Messaging: the homework benefit opens the conversation; the learning promise gives it substance.**

**One-liner**

> Room to Reason is an AI tutor that helps with tonight’s math, teaches the missing steps, and checks what your child can do independently.

**Parent-facing paragraph**

> When your child gets stuck, the next explanation matters. Room to Reason starts with the homework in front of them, helps them work through it, and makes time for the foundations underneath. Later checks show what they can do independently. Your report separates help, practice, and demonstrated learning—including what still needs attention. We’re starting with the mathematics that prepares children for algebra, at $49 a month.

**Recommended brand line:** *Make room for understanding.*

Other useful lines, all creative proposals rather than cleared slogans:

- *Help today. Build understanding.*
- *Good questions belong here.*
- *Start with what’s in front of you.*

| Parent objection | Honest answer |
|---|---|
| **“It’s AI, not a person.”** | “Yes. Your child works with an AI tutor, and it can make mistakes. The service is designed around teaching, reviewed material, and clear records of learning. It does not provide a human tutor’s judgment, relationship, or supervision.” |
| **“Will it just do the homework?”** | “The lesson asks your child to take steps, explain, and try again. Hints and worked examples may be part of teaching. We record that as helped work; independent learning requires a separate check. We cannot guarantee that a child never obtains an answer elsewhere.” |
| **“Why is it so cheap?”** | “Software delivers the tutoring, and the founder is funding the company’s fixed costs to keep the launch price accessible. The plan costs $49 with stated usage limits. That is a pricing commitment; exact delivery costs are still being measured.” |

The launch message should never claim measured break-even, unlimited tutoring, proven learning gains, or completed COPPA compliance before those statements are substantiated.

The remaining adoption gate is concrete: a live USPTO exact-and-similar-name screen in classes **41 and 9**, related-services review including class **42**, authoritative `.com` status and registrar terms, and parent/teen pronunciation and interpretation checks. **Room to Reason is the name I would take into that gate.**
