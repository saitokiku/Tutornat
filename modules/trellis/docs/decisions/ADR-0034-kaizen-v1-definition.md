# Kaizen v1, in Manny's answers

Five grill answers on 2026-09-11 define the first product:
1. Neither repo is the product; both are attempts. Kaizen-AI (real tutors system) is the more mature base; KaizenEdu was an isolated SaaS-first attempt (18:58).
2. "Private education" means supplemental first, then the infrastructure other providers run on; school, brick-and-mortar camps and a US-government relationship come much later (19:45).
3. The first product is the AI tutor alone, at or above human-tutor efficacy, with a human loop as an organization around it and a beautiful UI; real tutors are a separate branch that recommends the product; trellis is the growth record; primary-education automation is the far horizon (20:59).
4. 13+ for now (23:18).
5. Parents pay directly; national, online subscription; no parallel public-funds workstream: "A only focus" (23:23).

Consequences the PM acts on: SPEC v0.1 is written for this product and nothing else; the Texas rails research (RESEARCH.md) is kept, not pursued; the KaizenEdu engine and Kaizen-AI's record model and research are the reuse candidates (index); the efficacy bar is stated as a measured effect against an external instrument, with the research's 0.44 SD in-person vs 0.08 SD virtual pooled figures as the reference; legal scope for v1 is the 13+ band (SB 243-class disclosure duties, parent-owned accounts) with the under-13 gate deferred. Superseded within the Kaizen program: STRATEGY v0.2's Austin-club-first sequence and its "1:1 tutoring not sold, not built" rule (the founder's decision today reverses it for the AI product).
