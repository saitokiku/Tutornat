# "The homework turn into the demo"

Manny, 2026-09-12 15:55 CDT, iMessage, answering the grill follow-up — when a parent buys help for tonight's algebra
but the real gap is fractions, does the tutor rebuild the foundation first (A) or help with the
homework and repair underneath (B):

> "B for sure, it's what the demands today problem is, now we have the customer we can keep them, the homework turn into the demo. People value grades more than actual understanding, we take advantage of that to bring them in and keep them on the hook by actually deliver results but in the meantime we also grow the humans intellect, and actually is the hidden mission to produce well base formed human for the future"

**The model, in his terms:** sell to the demand that exists (grades, tonight's homework), keep them by
actually delivering that result, and use the time bought to build the thing they did not ask for
(understanding). The homework is simultaneously the product, the demo and the diagnostic.

**What this overturns:** Astra's recommendation was A — enter on the prerequisite gap and turn away
families who will not wait ([202609121548-manny-k1-algebra-readiness-conditional](202609121548-manny-k1-algebra-readiness-conditional.md) is still
A as the *subject*; this is about the *motion*). He has chosen the opposite motion. K1's subject
answer stands; the wedge is now "fix tonight, repair underneath".

**What it puts under strain, and must be designed against:**
1. **The mastery law.** A skill counts only on unassisted, delayed evidence — and homework help is
   assisted by definition. Week 1 is all help and no proof. The parent report has to tell that truth
   without looking empty.
2. **The free-alternative comparison.** Homework help is what a free general chatbot already does.
   Entering there means competing where our moat is weakest, so the repair underneath has to become
   visible fast.
3. **Honesty of the promise.** "Take advantage of that" is a positioning decision, not a licence to
   misdescribe. What we sell must be what we deliver; the hidden mission can be unadvertised without
   being untrue.
