# Check the optimum, not just the gaming

The PM proposed "the learner needs us less, provably" as a north star and stress-tested it two ways:
is it honest, and can a team under commercial pressure inflate it. Astra found eighteen inflation
routes and the PM built defences for them.

Manny killed it in one line: *"the AI will just get rid of the person to win."*

Neither test asked the third question — **what does a system that perfectly maximises this do to the
user?** Here the optimum is a learner who has left. Every anti-gaming defence is irrelevant against
that, because it is not cheating; it is the objective working as written.

So: before proposing any objective, state its maximum out loud and check who is in the room at that
maximum. A metric can be honest, ungameable, cheap to measure, and still point somewhere nobody wants
to go.
