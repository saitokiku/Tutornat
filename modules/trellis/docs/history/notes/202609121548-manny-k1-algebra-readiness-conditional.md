# "I guess A, I wanna know where it's worth it the problem is solvable and demand is real"

Manny, 2026-09-12 15:49 CDT, iMessage, answering grill Q1 (which parent problem deserves the entire first release,
even if we turn other families away):

> "I guess A, I wanna know where it's worth it the problem is solvable and demand is real"

**A = missing math foundations blocking algebra** (fractions → ratios → linear equations), over
B) Algebra 1 itself and C) reading and writing.

Read it as a **conditional answer, not a settled one.** "I guess" plus three conditions is him telling
us what would actually convince him:
1. **Worth it** — the problem is big enough to build a company on.
2. **Solvable** — an AI tutor can actually fix a prerequisite gap, not just get tonight's answer right.
3. **Demand is real** — parents already pay to fix this, knowingly.

Conditions 2 and 3 are research orders, scoped to algebra readiness rather than tutoring in general:
RO-8 (does AI teaching move *unaided transfer* on prerequisite gaps — primary trials, teens separated
from college students) and RO-7 (what parents of 12–15 year olds pay to fix math foundations, and what
they believe they are buying). Both run on Astra workers per his 15:37 instruction.

SPEC §8 K1 carries the conditional answer; it is not closed until those land.
