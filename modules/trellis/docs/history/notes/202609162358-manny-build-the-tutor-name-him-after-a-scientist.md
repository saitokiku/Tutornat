# "Get the tutor built, I wanna call him nikola or something like that"

**Manny, 2026-09-16, at this keyboard, minutes after being told both chosen names are blocked:**

> "Get the tutor built, I wanna call him nikola or something like that honor someone or the woman
> who found dna or something"

Two instructions in one line.

**1. Build.** E1 is dispatched on this word — the first builder since the repository was created on
09-13. "Budget informs, never refuses a direct ask" (ADR-0021-budget-informs-never-refuses *(PM vault: `30-decisions/ADR-0021-budget-informs-never-refuses`)*);
the dispatch gate read GO anyway.

**2. The name — a direction, not yet a name.** He is no longer choosing between Kaizen and Trellis.
He wants the *tutor* — "him" — named to **honor a person**: Nikola (Tesla), or "the woman who found
DNA" — Rosalind Franklin, whose X-ray diffraction image (Photo 51) established the double helix and
whose credit was taken by others. Read as taste: a name with a story a child can be told, and a
quiet correction of a historical wrong in Franklin's case.

What this settles: the tutor is a *character* with a person's name; the company/product brand
(Kaizen Academy LLC, the blocked K6 marks) is a separate question he did not answer here.
What it does not settle: which person, or whether the tutor's name is also the product's. A first
name used as a character inside a product is lighter trademark weight than a brand, but it is
not zero — Nikola is a live mark for vehicles and Rosalind is used by a few edtech products, so a
screen runs before it is printed anywhere.

Earlier direction still holds: the trellis metaphor stays in the data model even though the name is
gone ([ADR-0051-name-is-trellis](../../decisions/ADR-0051-name-is-trellis.md)); nothing is registered, printed or deployed under
any name until cleared ([202609162351-both-chosen-names-are-blocked-in-education](202609162351-both-chosen-names-are-blocked-in-education.md)).
