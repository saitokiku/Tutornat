# Both chosen names are blocked in education

Two names were chosen and both failed clearance in the same class we would have to file in.

**Kaizen.** Kaizen Institute Ltd. holds live class-41 registrations — 4072332 (education and
training, renewed 2021) and 1441520 (renewed 2017). UAB already distributes learning software
called Kaizen Education. Adding "Edu" does not establish separation.

**Trellis**, chosen by Manny on 2026-09-12 in [ADR-0051-name-is-trellis](../../decisions/ADR-0051-name-is-trellis.md), is worse
placed, not better. TRELLIS reg. 5418625 (Learn-It Systems, LLC) is live for **special education
courses for children** at kindergarten through middle school, and Trellis Apps already ships a US
AI learning coach called Trellis Academy, including to under-13s. That is a direct education
conflict, not a distant one.

So the argument in ADR-0051 — that the trellis metaphor was already in the data model — stands as
product reasoning and is dead as a name. The ADR stays accepted-then-blocked; it is superseded on
the name only.

**What this does and does not block.** Nothing may be registered, printed or deployed under either
name. `kaizenedu` is a repository name and `kaizenedu.net` is a domain Manny owns; neither is a
brand. Engine work, the spec and the evidence model never referenced the name, so E1 is unaffected.

RO-12 screened eight alternatives and advanced **Lessonford** and **Lessonwick** on available exact
.com and no demonstrated conflicting live mark. Neither is represented as legally cleared. This is
a taste question and a legal one, and it is Manny's: it goes to him as K6-reopened.
