## Branding kit

it. Apply it to the tutor persona (Nikola / Rosalind) and to refreshing BRAND.md.

## The time box is a checkpoint, not a guillotine
The Meeseeks rule (fleet-dispatch *(PM vault: `45-areas/fleet-dispatch`)*) said: past the box, stop and file partial work.
His amendment: **at the box, check the worker; then either re-guide it (narrow, redirect, extend
with what stands) or mark the work complete if it is good enough.** Good enough, reviewed, beats
perfect and unfinished. The paired blind review is unchanged — "done" is still a claim — but the
bar for accepting an increment is "does it hold and is it evidenced", not "is it everything".

Applied tonight: the E1 builder has a checkpoint armed at 55 minutes (00:53 CDT), ten minutes
before its 01:03 hard stop, so the check happens before the runtime kills anything.
