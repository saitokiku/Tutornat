# "grill me for it always and show me what you're working on"

Manny, 2026-09-11 18:49 CDT, iMessage, verbatim: "Finish the queue I sent through the PM portal, also grill me for it always and show me what you're working on and what you intend to do and how and also live view while it's being worked on (shell output etc)"

Standing instruction, three parts. (1) Grill is the default for any new piece of work he hands over, not a meeting he has to call: before research or a build aims at something, ask him the questions that would waste the most work if guessed. (2) Every wake that does work opens with what I am working on, what I intend to do and how, before the work, not only a report after. (3) A live view exists and he is told where it is: the Watch surface streams the session transcript including shell output (STATE.md §Surfaces (`../../STATE.md#surfaces`)); the Workers panel shows worker log tails. Applied first in [ADR-0033-kaizen-is-the-active-product](../../decisions/ADR-0033-kaizen-is-the-active-product.md); extends ADR-0006-interrupt-cadence *(PM vault: `30-decisions/ADR-0006-interrupt-cadence`)* from taste to work itself.
