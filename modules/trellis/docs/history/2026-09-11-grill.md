# Grill — Kaizen (started 2026-09-11 18:56 CDT, one question at a time)

Purpose: make the PM's model of what Manny means by "the final product" and "the private education guy" match reality before market, legal and SPEC work aims at it. His standing instruction: "grill me for it always" ([202609112349-manny-grill-always-show-work-live-view](notes/202609112349-manny-grill-always-show-work-live-view.md)).

**Q1 (18:56 CDT, board Needs-you lane + text):** Which one is "the final product"? A) Kaizen AI as is: Austin in-person club + AI companion · B) Natural Tutor: the AI tutor, national, software-first · C) one product: the AI tutor plus a verified learning record, with Austin as the human lab.

**A1 (18:58 CDT, iMessage, verbatim):** "Neither yet is the final product, they are both attempts, but the Kaizen AI repo with the real tutors system is the more mature one the kaizen edu is a Saas first isolated attempt"

What changed: both repos are attempts, not the product; Kaizen-AI (human tutors + system) is the mature base; KaizenEdu was an isolated SaaS-first try. The final product is to be defined, not chosen between the two.

**Q2 (19:00 CDT, text + board):** What does "private education" mean for the final product? A) a school (full-time enrollment, ESA school tier, accreditation and facility law) · B) supplemental (tutoring, after-school, enrichment for kids already in a school; 529/ESA tutoring rails; vendor rules) · C) infrastructure (the learning system other private-ed providers run on; our own sites as proof). PM's read: B now, built to become C.

**A2 (19:45 CDT, iMessage, verbatim):** "It's more b then C but school comes way down the line I have plans for brick and mortar camps etc and joining with the US govt later"

What changed: confirmed B → C as the sequence; a school is far later, not a non-goal; brick-and-mortar camps are a planned physical format; a US government relationship is a later-stage goal (the STRATEGY v0.2 "Kaizen Gov" idea has a live owner). Research scope now: supplemental-education market and rails first (529, ESA tutoring tiers, §25F SGOs), provider-platform path second, camps and public-sector later.

**Q3 (19:50 CDT, text + board):** In the first product, who runs the learning loop? A) Human tutors deliver; the AI is the record and the companion (Kaizen-AI's shape: standing seats, in person) · B) The AI tutor delivers 1:1 online; humans sign off assessments, handle escalation and accountability (KaizenEdu's engine on Kaizen-AI's record) · C) Both from day one: in-person seats in Austin run on the same AI loop as online. PM's recommendation: B, with Manny as the human teacher in the loop and small Austin cohorts as the lab; C is the goal only after the AI loop proves it moves an external test.

**A3 (20:59 CDT, iMessage, verbatim):** "First product AI / algorithm only but max efficacy of software human loop under an organization beautifying UI . Real tutors will be run  as a separate branch that will suggest to use this product and if you have both human and software you're in full harmony and trellis can help you grow but focus on getting the AI itself as a perfect product on the efficacy of if not better that of human tutors and try to make it for kids as young as possible I'm also trying to automate primary education early on but that's very long term future"

What changed: the first product is the AI tutor alone (option B, stronger than the PM's recommendation: no in-person cohort in v1); a human loop exists as an organization around the software, not as the delivery; the UI must be beautiful; real tutors are a separate branch that recommends the AI product; "both" is the harmony state and the trellis (Kaizen-AI's skill graph + immutable evidence) is the growth record; the success bar is efficacy at or above human tutors (the research puts in-person tutoring at 0.44 SD and virtual at 0.08 SD pooled — that gap is the product's thesis); the learner should be as young as possible; automating primary education is the very long term.

**Q4 (21:03 CDT, text + board):** How young in v1? A) 13+ now: the shipped tutor, parent-owned accounts exist; SB 243 disclosure duties are known and buildable · B) 8–12 from day one: COPPA verifiable parental consent, model-provider under-13 terms, CA SB 1119 audit exposure, no counsel signoff yet (the repo's own under-13 gate) · C) ship 13+ first on the same engine, build the under-13 release as the immediate second step gated on counsel signoff. PM's recommendation: C — the efficacy proof comes faster with teens on the engine that runs today, and the under-13 build is a legal workstream, not a product detour.

**A4 (23:18 CDT, iMessage, verbatim):** "Okay let's go 13 plus for now"

What changed: v1 serves 13+ (option A, "for now"): the shipped tutor's age band, parent-owned accounts, SB 243 duties to build. Under-13 is not scheduled; it stays a later release gated on counsel, with "as young as possible" (A3) as the direction.

**Q5 (23:22 CDT, text + board):** Who pays for v1, and where does it launch? A) Parents directly, national, online subscription (KaizenEdu's current shape; fastest to first dollar; the efficacy proof runs on paying families) · B) Texas public-funds families first: TEFA vendor approval and 529-eligible framing before launch (price parity binds; §529's outside-the-home and online questions are unresolved; 10-business-day payment) · C) Schools and districts (Khan has set the price floor at $10–15 per student per year; Nerdy just left this market). PM's recommendation: A for launch, with B run in parallel as a registration and research workstream so Texas money can flow without shaping v1.

**A5 (23:23 CDT, iMessage, verbatim):** "A only focus"

What changed: v1 sells to parents directly, national, online subscription; no Texas public-funds workstream in parallel (the PM's "B alongside" is declined: focus). The rails research stays on record for later.

## Close (23:30 CDT)
Five questions, five answers, no contradictions between answers; one contradiction with the repos resolved (neither is the product).
