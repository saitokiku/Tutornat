# Kaizen RO-2 — minors launch gates

Research cutoff: September 12, 2026. Scope: United States, 50 states plus DC; parent-purchased tutoring, learner accounts ages 13–17, text/voice/whiteboard, persistent learning records, parent reports, safety escalation. No school procurement, under-13 launch, territories, or international launch assumed. This is a primary-text applicability report, not a legal opinion or a compliance certification.

**Finding: the supplied record does not establish eligibility for a national minor beta. “SB 243-class duties plus parental consent” is not a national legal or contractual permission.** The statutes regulate different actors, ages, data uses, and features; some duties cannot be consented away. Google’s published Gemini API and Google Cloud generative-AI terms independently restrict under-18 applications. OpenAI and Anthropic describe conditional API use with minors, but this research does not establish that Kaizen has satisfied their conditions. ## Reading the matrix

**H** = exact primary text inspected; high confidence in the stated text/date, not necessarily Kaizen's classification. **M** = supported interpretation or an official explanatory source, with the inference identified. **U** = unresolved or insufficiently verified; not clearance.

**J?** in an injunction column means this research did not establish a controlling injunction or certify the current docket. It does **not** mean “no injunction exists.” **Contract** means the row concerns vendor permission, not a statute. Dates are operative/compliance dates unless explicitly identified as enactment, publication, or document-version dates. A row's source ID links to a passage below; dates and confidence apply to the row's claims.

**Consent must be recorded by purpose and authority.** Account payment/contract assent, parent permission required by a vendor, a teen's own statutory data-processing consent, FERPA disclosure consent, biometric consent, and emergency/incident disclosures are different questions. Where a statute prohibits a processing purpose, a consent checkbox cannot create the missing permission. This is a synthesis of F2, F6, V1–V6 and P1–P8, not a claim that one universal consent form exists.

### Counsel questions that the sources do not close

1. **Definition and beta boundary:** Which implemented modes satisfy each relationship, general-public, school-setting, narrow-topic, or social-service test? Does invitation-only availability change any particular statute's public-availability element? An exclusion under one law does not decide another.
2. **Consenter and age evidence:** Which decisions belong to the 13–17-year-old, the parent, the contracting adult, the school, or an eligible student? What evidence establishes age and parental authority without creating unnecessary biometric/identity retention?
3. **Necessary versus optional processing:** Are weekly reports, longitudinal skill models, safety classifiers, human review, analytics, vendor abuse logs, product research, fine-tuning and cross-product memory necessary for the requested tutoring service under each applicable text? A provider's “no training” commitment does not resolve application-side research or retention.
4. **Parent transparency and teen confidentiality:** What may be disclosed in progress reports, full transcripts or crisis notices? Which law requires a monitoring signal? What happens if a minor objects, revokes optional consent, becomes an eligible student or ages out? The record does not support giving every parent unrestricted access to every teen utterance.
5. **Safety operations:** What evidence satisfies reasonable measures, evidence-based methods and industry standards? How do recurring crisis prompts, out-of-hours response, therapist-like output, child-abuse duties and emergency disclosure interact? No inspected statute establishes Kaizen's proposed human response time as a national legal safe harbor.
6. **Deletion and holds:** How do statutory deletion rights, vendor logs, backups, research copies, learning-evidence history and specific incident preservation fit together? California SB1119's future three-year incident hold is not permission for indefinite retention of every student's entire record.
7. **Entity and geography:** Which operating/parent/affiliate entity is controller, operator or processor? What revenue/data thresholds actually apply? What locations count when parent, learner and school are in different states? Territories and non-US residents were not cleared.
8. **Current enforceability:** What are the current mandates, stays, remand orders and successor-law consequences in Texas/California and other challenged regimes? A judicial invalidation of third-party content filtering does not resolve AI-generated content liability.
9. **Contract hierarchy:** Does the executed provider agreement incorporate the inspected online versions, and is there a documented exception to any minor restriction? Which ancillary APIs have separate terms? Parent consent cannot supply an absent vendor contract right.
10. **National completion:** Which unverified inventory entries, state student-privacy laws, audio-recording rules, mental-health statutes and consumer/security laws reach this exact service? Research gaps are unresolved questions, not negative legal findings.
