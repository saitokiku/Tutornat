**The first two rows are the important finding: someone else has live registrations for bare KAIZEN in education, including registration 4072332’s training facilities and education-related conferences/seminars.** Its coverage is broader than the older business-management lecture registration. Neither is limited to a logo. Adding the education abbreviation “Edu” does not establish adequate separation; counsel must assess the whole marks, actual services and customer overlap. Identical class numbers alone do not establish infringement: the aerospace record covers drone imaging, and the other three software records concern healthcare data, drilling and factories. [USPTO likelihood-of-confusion guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion).

Do not count cancelled coverage: serial 77606883’s **classes 9 and 42 are cancelled**, and “computer education training services” is bracket-deleted from class 41. The table reproduces only its surviving class-41 goods, while showing every class’s cancellation label. The three remaining bare KAIZEN class-9 records are live, including one pending application. “Live” is not synonymous with registered.
