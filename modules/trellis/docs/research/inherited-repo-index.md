# Kaizen index — both repositories, 2026-09-11

**What exists today.** Two separate, working Next.js products on Vercel, both pointed at the domain kaizenedu.net. **Kaizen-AI** (`91af9e4`, main last touched 2026-09-03) is a complete in-person club storefront and operations system: 25 pages, 67 API routes, 38 Supabase migrations, 60 unit suites, a placement diagnostic, a club schedule and booking flow, a Director's console and a founder's funnel board — with selling held shut by `app_settings.club_enabled`, seeded `false`. **KaizenEdu / Natural Tutor** (`20a971b`, 2026-09-05) is a voice-first 1:1 AI tutor built on a merged copy of OpenMAIC: 24 product pages, 35 product API routes, a streaming turn engine, on-device VAD with 300 ms barge-in, a whiteboard that draws while the tutor talks, graded checks, parent-owned accounts with consent plumbing, and a live health route that currently answers `ok: true`. It is the one that can take a learner through a session today.

**The through-line.** One claim, carried in both repos and unchanged across every rewrite: the AI is not the chatbot, the AI is the record — a skill counts only on unassisted, verified, delayed evidence, and no surface may score assisted work as mastery. Kaizen-AI encodes it as the trellis (`kc` + `kc_edge` lattice, `evidence` rows immutable by trigger since migration 0034) and `docs/ENGINE.md`. KaizenEdu encodes it as D17 and the two-tier student model (estimate, then confirmed only on an unassisted check at least 24 hours later). Everything else about the business changed at least twice — an AI-only SaaS, then a tutor marketplace, then an Austin club with a free AI, now a 1:1 AI tutor sold first — but that one sentence survived all of it.

**Where they overlap and contradict.** The engines overlap: both have a skill graph, a mastery ledger, a diagnostic, an item bank, intake, safety prompts and a parent report. They contradict on identity. Kaizen-AI's README says private 1:1 tutoring is "Not sold, and not built"; KaizenEdu *is* a 1:1 tutor and its `docs/MVP-REFERENCE.md` declares Kaizen-AI "a source archive. Nothing in it ships." Kaizen-AI still prices private 1:1 in `clubPricing.js` and renders those rates on `/terms`. Each repo has its own price authority (`clubPricing.js` vs `kaizen.config.ts`) and they are unrelated, so no single public price list spans both.

**What a final product could reuse.** From KaizenEdu: the entire live tutor — turn engine, voice pipeline, presence layer, session state machine, accounts and consent, cost ceilings, the five invariant tests, the 24-row claims ledger. From Kaizen-AI: the verified business research (`STRATEGY.md` v0.2 with its ✓/~/✗ source marks, the franchise and SB 243 findings, the TEFA/529/§25F rail analysis), the trellis schema and mastery law, 38 hardened migrations with RLS, the CI claims and price guards, the demo course materials, and the legal review queue. The cheapest path is one product — the tutor — carrying Kaizen-AI's record model and research, with the club retained as an offline business that sells seats into the same record.

**Top 5 risks.** (1) Six live-shaped provider keys are committed in `KaizenEdu/.env.local` on a remote repository and the repo's own notes say to rotate them; they are still there. (2) KaizenEdu's product is 7% of its own codebase — the other 93% is OpenMAIC, whose root `package.json` and `LICENSE` still declare MIT/THU-MAIC, with 82 MB of upstream demo assets and a `.git` of 111 MB, and no `upstream` remote configured to run the weekly merge the rules require. (3) Legal is unfinished in both: 26 uncleared items in Kaizen-AI (item 17 gates revenue), no `compliance/signoff.md` for KaizenEdu's under-13 gate, an unread Gemini terms question about under-18 use, and a franchise finding against Kaizen Certified. (4) Kaizen-AI's newest code is 22 unmerged commits on `claude/strategy-progress-assessment-rb6eju`, which deletes `legacy/` and reports that "the mastery record has almost no way to be written" — a correctness claim main has not absorbed. (5) KaizenEdu's own gates are past: Gate 1 was 2026-09-17, email is still unconfigured and blocks password reset, parent handoff, safety paging and the weekly report, and zero of 118 item-bank items are signed off by a person.
