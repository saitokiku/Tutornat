**What form has a chance — ranked, not declared cleared**

No form is cleared by this screen. The following ranking is a commercial/clearance triage judgment, not a guarantee of registrability. Adding a descriptive word or house mark does not automatically solve confusion. [TMEP §1207.01](https://tmep.uspto.gov/RDMS/TMEP/print?href=TMEP-1200d1e5044.html&version=current).

1. **A materially distinctive compound** — e.g. **Trellis & Finch**, used as the entire brand, or a more changed coined form such as **Trellisora**. These are the only naming directions here worth sending for further clearance. Exact candidate queries returned zero live federal hits; quoted web searches returned no exact branded business among returned results; the two .com RDAP checks returned 404. **Those facts do not clear either name.** The continued TRELLIS element still needs analysis, and neither may later be shortened to bare Trellis on the assumption that compound clearance covers it.
2. **Trellis Ed** — weak. “Ed” identifies the service and there is existing Trellis.Ed educator training plus the broader education conflicts.
3. **The Trellis** — weak. The article adds little separation; The Trellis School already operates.
4. **Trellis Learning** — reject as the straightforward workaround. An exact education operator already exists; a purchasable .com does not cure it.
5. **Trellis Academy** — reject as the straightforward workaround. Existing Texas school, US AI learning coach and Canadian tutoring domain.

Ranks 2–5 are all no-go choices for an uncomplicated launch; their relative order does not imply any is safe. If Manny means **the exact bare name must stay**, the path is legal rights resolution, not another suffix.

**What Manny has to do to own a usable form**

1. Give US trademark counsel this dossier and the actual national children's-tutor scope. The first questions are **86708361**, **97269546**, **Trellis Apps, LLC**, and **Trellis Learning**, followed by the Texas academy and other related users: priority, actual use, scope, existing agreements, and likelihood of confusion. A professional-services limitation in one registration does not dispose of the separate children's users.
2. If keeping Trellis, establish whether a viable coexistence/consent or license can cover the intended national service, or whether a properly structured acquisition of relevant rights and associated goodwill is feasible. **One owner's consent cannot clear everyone else's rights.** No owner's willingness or price was established. Otherwise, clear a materially different compound as a whole.
3. After that clearance decision, secure the corresponding domain and file truthful, product-specific applications: **41 for tutoring/education, 42 for hosted software, and 9 if downloadable software will actually be offered**. Use intent-to-use if appropriate; registration later requires proper evidence of use. [USPTO filing basis](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/apply/basis). Determine exact identifications with counsel; avoid claiming the entire class.
