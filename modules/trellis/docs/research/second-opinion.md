## K2 — Independent strategy

- [verified] Manny chose AI-only delivery, parents direct, national and online, with **“A only focus”** rejecting parallel public-funds work. `vault/30-decisions/ADR-0034-kaizen-v1-definition.md`.
- [opinion] **The wedge is underspecified.** “AI tutor for 13+” describes distribution and eligibility, not a reason to buy. Enter around **one recurring learning failure in the chosen subject**, with a bounded promise: the learner can solve unfamiliar problems independently next week.
- [opinion] Keep subject K1 with Manny. Within that subject, recruit learners with a demonstrated prerequisite gap and an immediate reason to fix it. National recruitment can still target one narrow problem.
- [opinion] **The mechanic:** attempt → identify competing explanations for the error → choose a task that distinguishes them → brief instruction → learner teaches the idea back → unfamiliar application → delayed retrieval. The tutor’s objective is to make its help unnecessary.
- [recalled] Retrieval, spacing, worked-example fading and self-explanation have substantial learning-science foundations. Training knowledge; high confidence in the general mechanisms, uncertain transfer to this particular AI implementation. Their combination here is an experiment, not a proven invention.
- [opinion] Use Manny first for adversarial teaching scenarios, confusing explanations and interface friction. His performance cannot establish teen efficacy or willingness to return.
- [opinion] **Cost of the wrong wedge:** a few weeks of narrow content and recruitment are recoverable. A broad tutor, persistent persona and infrastructure platform built before retention evidence are much harder to recover.

- [opinion] **The record is necessary plumbing, not yet a moat.** A frontier lab can reproduce a schema and report. Tutoring brands can add reporting to existing relationships. School vendors already compete for institutional trust.
- [opinion] The stronger asset is a **validated intervention policy**: which action helps which misconception, under which conditions, with measured delayed transfer. Couple that with calibrated assessments, trusted attestation and distribution that brings learners back.
- [opinion] Raw interaction volume is not that asset. Training rights, population bias, outcome validity and replicated improvement determine whether the data are useful.
- [opinion] The infrastructure step begins when another provider willingly uses the record and can verify its claims. Selling records into admissions or accountability is a separate buyer hypothesis—not an escape from competition.

- [opinion] **Price recommendation for existing K3: $29/month**, one learner, a defined learning program, approximately three 20-minute sessions weekly plus short retrieval checks. Make voice allowances explicit; avoid an unlimited-compute promise.
- [opinion] Sell reduced struggle and demonstrated independence. Parent reports support renewal; parents should not need to understand a mastery ledger to understand the purchase.
- [opinion] Under K3’s illustrative cost scenario, five learner-hours plus $4 allocated payment/hosting/support expense leave roughly **$20 monthly contribution**. Start with a **≤$40 CAC target** and two-month payback; these are operating targets, not observed economics.
- [opinion] Do not assume indefinite subscription life. Successful learners may finish the problem they bought us to solve. Distinguish graduation, seasonal pauses, dissatisfaction and nonuse; measure next-skill uptake without manufacturing dependence.

- [opinion] **Efficacy ambition: human-tutor parity. Initial company gate: repeatable independent learning plus repeat use.** A historical 0.44 threshold risks optimizing the study rather than the product.
- [opinion] Measure delayed transfer, assistance needed, retention of prior skills, completed dose, learner willingness to return, parent effort and all-in cost. Minutes chatting and immediate correctness are diagnostics.
- [opinion] Human parity eventually requires a concurrent, credible human-tutoring comparator at specified dose, with a justified noninferiority margin. Failure to find a significant difference is not sufficient evidence of parity.
