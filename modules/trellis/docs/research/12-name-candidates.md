**Recommendation: Lessonford and Lessonwick.** Their exact .com domains were confirmed available for new registration, and the searches below found no demonstrated conflicting live mark. Lessonford has one relevant live search hit requiring follow-up; Lessonwick has a registered sound-alike domain. Neither is represented as legally cleared.

Eight candidates are ranked below: two advance, four are reserves, and two remain on hold. All eight passed the exact-domain purchase-availability check. I cannot honestly label all eight adoption-ready. The related names and language concerns are included so the weaker choices are reviewable, not hidden behind a “no results” claim.

**Checked: September 12, 2026, America/Chicago (CDT).** Exact check timestamps are preserved in the linked evidence. This is a time-boxed US federal and public-web screen. No domain was registered, added to a cart, purchased, or reserved; no one was contacted.

The fixed home remains **kaizenedu.net**, and the entity remains **Kaizen Academy LLC**. The candidates describe a broad education offering for parents of learners about 8–17. They contain no independence/“needs us less” thesis, AI terminology, coding cue, or maths-only restriction. A product name plus “by Kaizen Academy” is the proposed naming relationship. A candidate .com would be optional protection or a redirect to the chosen .net, not a replacement-domain recommendation.

## What “checked” means

- **Exact .com:** queried Verisign’s authoritative .com RDAP service, then confirmed the exact domain through Porkbun’s public domain-search flow. Every shortlisted domain returned RDAP HTTP 404 and Porkbun `AVAILABLE`, `type: registration`, `premium: 0`, `inCart: 0`. This establishes the registrar offered a normal new registration at the recorded instant; it does not reserve it. Registrar responses and registry responses are linked per candidate.
- **Near-misses:** “Registered” means Verisign returned a domain object with HTTP 200. “No RDAP record” means HTTP 404; purchase availability of those near-misses is **unverified** because they were not each registrar-checked. All near-miss checks occurred on September 12, 2026. Registration alone says nothing about whether a website operates there.
- **USPTO:** used the current **Trademark Search** system and its public search backend, not a third-party trademark aggregator. TESS was retired on November 30, 2023. Exact and spaced-name searches covered all classes and live/dead statuses. Expanded searches required a live record and class **9, 41, or 42**; class 42 was added because hosted software can fall there. The field tags and exact query strings are recorded below. [USPTO TESS retirement notice](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USPTO/bulletins/3754ddd), [official search field-tag guide](https://www.uspto.gov/sites/default/files/documents/TM-FederalTrademarkSearching-FieldTags-handout.pdf), [USPTO software classification examples](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/maintain/amending-your-registration-s-goodsservices-when).
- **Search reliability:** every final candidate query returned HTTP 200, no timeout, and all its reported hits. A known KHAN ACADEMY live record was returned by a positive-control query. An earlier HTTP 429 batch was superseded after the server’s wait interval; errors were never counted as zero results. Positive-control response (`raw/name-checks/trademarks/final-positive-control.json`).
- **Limits:** a zero result is only a statement about the named search. Comprehensive phonetic/design, state-register, common-law, international, app-store and handle clearance is **unverified**. Pronunciation/spelling assessments are researcher judgments; no parents or native speakers were contacted. The report identifies specific language findings where verified and marks the rest unverified.

The surviving pool clusters around “Lesson” because it gives parents an immediate education cue while staying subject-neutral. This is a deliberately narrow, checked set rather than eight unrelated naming styles.
