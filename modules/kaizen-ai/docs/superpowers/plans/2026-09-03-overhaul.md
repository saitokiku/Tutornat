# The overhaul, in phases

*2026-09-03. Supersedes `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-03-wave3-frontend.md`,
which was written hours earlier against a narrower business. Canonical business
doc is still `docs/STRATEGY.md` (v0.2) and its §3 diagram; canonical sequence for
the club's go-live is still `docs/RELEASE_PLAN.md` (v2). This is the engineering
programme that sits under both.*

## Why

Two waves made the product **truthful** — every claim carries a row, every room
prints its own timezone, every failed read says so — and they did it by
narrowing: private 1:1 cut, the marketplace gated shut, the storefront rebuilt
around the standing seat alone.

The business has now widened again, in a direction the codebase has never been
shaped for:

- **Five revenue lines**: 1v1, group (Hall / Clinic), the placement diagnostic at
  **$65**, the standing seat, and the AI tutor with its paid upgrade. This
  reverses Wave 2's cut of 1:1 and the marketplace.
- **Two populations**: under-13 children get an AI-tutor-only product. Today
  under-13 is blocked outright and logged as a COPPA trail;
  `MIN_STATED_AGE = 13` is a constant in `lib/server/context.js`.
- **The AI tutor stops being a chatbot**: natural voice, a face, and animated
  explanations of concepts via **OpenMAIC** (Tsinghua's Open Multi-Agent
  Interactive Classroom, MIT).

The frontend is **25,054 lines against a backend of 22,026**, carrying three
generations of UI primitive that all still ship. That gets collapsed — but
carefully, and not where a later phase is going to rebuild anyway.

Phases are ordered so the product stays sellable throughout, and so the tail
(Phase 8+) is a repeating engine rather than a finish line.

## Two defects this plan exists to prevent

Both were found by a sequencing review, both were about to be shipped by a
plausible-looking change.

**A price change that starts in code defrauds the customer.**
`app/api/diagnostic/route.js:357-364` computes `chargedCents()` from
`checkout.amount_total` — **Stripe is the authority on the charge**. Ship
`DIAGNOSTIC.priceCents 5900 → 6500` before creating the Stripe Price and
repointing `STRIPE_PRICE_DIAGNOSTIC`, and the storefront advertises $65 while the
card is charged $59 and `diagnostic_order.amount_cents` records 5900. **Operator
step first.**

**Lowering the under-13 floor alone admits a child to live 1:1 video.**
`app/api/family/children/route.js:179` writes `guardian_consent_at: nowIso` at
creation. `lib/server/family.js:122` is `if (studentProfile.guardian_consent_at)
return true;`. So a child created under a lowered floor at `:113` lands **already
consented**, and `guardianGateSatisfied` passes them through the gate whose whole
purpose is stopping a minor being alone in a video room with an adult. **The age
band must be a separate axis from consent, checked independently.** A satisfied
guardian gate must never imply human contact for the child band.

## What the guards actually do

Worth stating plainly, because the plan leans on them and one of them does less
than its reputation:

- `test/priceTruth.test.mjs` — real. Scans `app/` and `components/` **including
  comments**, plus a pinned list of docs. Removing `59` from its `CURRENT` set
  will break two *comments* (`app/admin/page.js:1797`,
  `app/api/admin/diagnostics/route.js:11`).
- `test/claims.test.mjs` — **a banned-phrase scanner**, six regexes. There is no
  mechanical link between a rendered claim and a `CLAIMS_MATRIX` row. "No public
  claim without a row" is a human rule with a partial backstop. Phases 6 and 7
  add the largest batch of new claims in the product's history; the linkage gets
  built in Phase 1 while the matrix is at a known-good 81 rows.
- `test/legalMarkers.test.mjs` — real. Fails on un-cleared `[ATTORNEY REVIEW]`.
- **32 of 60 test files read source as text.** This is why the design collapse
  precedes the admin split: `app/admin/page.js` is pinned by three of them,
  including `assert.match(adminPage, /<SeatsSection \/>/)`, which pins the mount
  site. Split second and those assertions get rewritten once instead of twice.

## Phase 7a — The face *(parallel with 7b)*
Voice shipped in 1.5, so the face comes here: a face without voice is a puppet.
**First commit is a reviewed CSP change** — `next.config.js` has no `worker-src`,
which falls through `child-src` to `default-src 'self'`, so blob: workers are
blocked and Live2D/three.js loaders spawn them. Narrow the change. 2D first;
3D is a kill-gated follow-on. Rule 6: a missing avatar asset renders "not
configured", never a broken canvas. An animated persona talking to a child is
exactly what `REVIEW_QUEUE` item 21 (California SB 243) is about.

## Phase 7b — OpenMAIC as an offline generator *(parallel with 7a)*
**Extract the pipeline, not the app.** Rejected: a vendored subpath (a
`tsconfig.json` at `web/` makes `next build` type-check the whole tree, LangGraph
enters the bundle, and OpenMAIC's 15-provider abstraction competes with
`lib/server/models.js`, the only place model choice may live); and an iframe
(`frame-src` allows only Stripe and Daily, and OpenMAIC's "interactive HTML
simulations" are *model-authored HTML* — not a thing to iframe on a minors-facing
product).

Instead: vendor it under `tools/openmaic/` (MIT), run it as a Node 22 job that
emits **scene JSON** validated against a schema Kaizen owns, store it, and render
with a player built from `components/ui/`. Model calls go through Anthropic on
Kaizen's key. No LangGraph in the bundle, no TS/JS question, no CSP change — and
**the artifact is reviewable before a child sees it**, which is what makes it
defensible for the 2b population. Anchor scene JSON to `kc` ids so a scene sits
on the trellis rather than floating beside it. Attribution in
`web/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, enforced by `scripts/checkNotices.mjs`.

## Phase 8+ — The loop
The plan ends in an engine, not a finish line. Three repeating additive tracks,
none re-opening a decided question:

- **(a)** one lattice-expansion phase per subject — `supabase/seed_kc.sql` and
  `seed_kc_algebra1.sql` already show the pattern;
- **(b)** one scene-library phase per unit, through the 7b generator;
- **(c)** one deepening phase per revenue line.

Optional fourth: the real Director role, once Phase 4 has proven the screens
separate cleanly.
