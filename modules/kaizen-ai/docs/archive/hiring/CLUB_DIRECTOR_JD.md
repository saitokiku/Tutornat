**Kaizen Academy · online, after-school · part-time hours, owner-level role**

## What Kaizen is

Kaizen is an online after-school academic club for students 13+. Real tutors
on a weekly schedule — the standing seat ($550/mo, 2 × 75 min a week, 1:4, in person), Homework Hall ($14), Subject Clinics ($20), private
1:1 — plus a free community session every single week and an AI study
companion between sessions. Think of it as academic primary care: a regular,
low-friction place where help happens early, before small gaps grow. We don't
promise grades; nobody honest can. We build the habit of steady, gradual
improvement — that's what *kaizen* means.

The product is built, deployed, and taking bookings. What it needs now is
the person who makes it real in a community: rooms that fill, tutors worth
trusting, parents who come back. That's this role.

## What you'll actually do (you will wear all five hats)

1. **Teach.** You personally run Homework Halls and the weekly free Community
   Hall, especially at the start. You are the quality bar and the face
   parents meet first.
2. **Build the tutor roster.** Recruit the first 5–8 tutors — likely from
   your own network — vet them, train them on our session standards, and cut
   fast and kindly when someone isn't right.
3. **Win the community.** PTA nights, teacher relationships, libraries,
   school-adjacent outreach. Our marketing budget is small on purpose; your
   credibility is the channel.
4. **Keep families.** The follow-up calls, the "want me to book Tuesdays for
   you?" notes, the second-no-show phone call. Retention is the whole
   business, and it lives in this hat.
5. **Own safety.** You're the first responder on our incident runbook and
   the moderation trainer. We serve minors on live video; this is the hat
   that outranks all others.

You'll run the operation against five numbers on a live dashboard — seats
per tutor-hour, first-to-second-session conversion, member visit
utilization, no-shows, refunds. Those numbers are your scoreboard, and you
will have full visibility into all of them, plus the real financials.

## Who this is for

- You've run an education operation: tutoring-center director (Mathnasium,
  Kumon, Sylvan or similar), teacher/department head, after-school or
  homeschool-co-op program director — or you've been the tutor every parent
  requests.
- Parents trust you on sight, and you have references from **families**, not
  just managers.
- You want ownership economics and a scoreboard, not a salary and a boss.
- You take child-safety obligations personally and seriously.
- You can commit real weekly hours in the after-school window (roughly 4–8 PM
  school nights) for at least a school year.

## How you're paid (summary — full plan shared before your trial)

- **Session pay from day one**: $22–30/hr for every session you teach.
- **Profit share**: a 25% share of operating profit (rising to 35% on hit
  milestones), distributed quarterly against fully transparent books.
- **Your share is yours**: take it as cash, or reinvest any portion to buy
  real equity at a pre-agreed price.
- Founder takes **no salary before your profit share is paid** in year one.
- Flexible hours, remote, founding-partner title, reference checks and
  liability insurance covered by us.

This is a build-something role with startup-shaped pay: modest guaranteed
money, meaningful ownership, complete transparency. If you need a full salary
now, this isn't the right year to join us — honestly stated up front.

## How to apply

Email a short note (no formal résumé needed) covering: (1) the education
operation you've run or the teaching you've done, (2) one story about
winning over a skeptical parent, and (3) your rough weekly availability
4–8 PM. Finalists are offered a **paid 30-day working trial** — you run real
sessions, and the dashboard does the judging.

*Kaizen Academy LLC. Tutors and staff are interviewed and approved by our
team; all roles working with students require reference and background
checks. We welcome applicants of every background.*
