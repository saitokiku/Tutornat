# Answer Key — HW3 Genetics

**Part A**
1. **Allele** = an alternative version of a gene. **Homozygous** = two identical alleles (PP or pp). **Heterozygous** = two different alleles (Pp).
2. Law of Segregation: the two alleles for a trait **separate during gamete formation**, so each gamete carries only one allele; they recombine at fertilization.
3. **Genotype** = the allele combination (e.g., Pp); **phenotype** = the observable trait (purple flowers). A Pp and a PP plant have different genotypes but the same purple phenotype.

**Part B**
4. Pp × pp → gametes P,p × p,p → **1 Pp : 1 pp** → genotype ratio 1:1; **phenotype ratio 1 purple : 1 white**.
5. Pp × Pp → 1 PP : 2 Pp : 1 pp → **3 purple : 1 white**; probability white = **1/4**.
6. The purple parent is **heterozygous (Pp)**. In a test cross with pp, a homozygous PP parent would give all purple offspring; getting ~50% white shows the purple parent carries a recessive p allele (Pp × pp → 1 purple : 1 white).

**Part C**
7. (a) **9 round-yellow : 3 round-green : 3 wrinkled-yellow : 1 wrinkled-green** (9:3:3:1).
   (b) Round (R_) = 3/4, green (yy) = 1/4 → 3/4 × 1/4 = **3/16**.
8. Wrinkled (rr) from Rr × Rr = **1/4**; yellow (Y_) from Yy × yy = **1/2**; product = 1/4 × 1/2 = **1/8**.
