# Answer Key — Final Exam (Units 1–8)

**Section I:** 1) **B**  2) **C**  3) **A**  4) **C**  5) **B**  6) **B**  7) **C**

**Section II**
8. **DNA → RNA → protein.** Replication copies DNA; **transcription** makes mRNA from a DNA template (in the nucleus); **translation** builds a protein from the mRNA at the ribosome. Product of transcription = mRNA; product of translation = polypeptide/protein.
9. Hardy–Weinberg (no evolution) requires: **no mutation, no natural selection, no gene flow (migration), random mating, and a very large population**. Any of mutation, selection, gene flow, genetic drift, or non-random mating can change allele frequencies.
10. **Light reactions** occur in the **thylakoid membranes**, use light to make **ATP and NADPH** (and release O₂ from water). **Calvin cycle** occurs in the **stroma**, uses that ATP and NADPH to fix CO₂ into **sugar (G3P/glucose)**; it does not use light directly.

**Section III**
11. (a) All F1 are **Gg**, gray. (b) Gg × Gg → 1 GG : 2 Gg : 1 gg → **3 gray : 1 black**; probability black = **1/4**. (c) The gray parent is **Gg (heterozygous)**. In a test cross with gg, a GG parent would give all gray offspring; getting ~50% black shows the gray parent carries a recessive g allele (Gg × gg → 1 gray : 1 black).
