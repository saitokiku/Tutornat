# One-Shot Semester Intake — zero to a full graded dashboard

These are **paste-ready blocks for Kaizen's own magic-box intake**. Drop a block
into the intake box (onboarding, or the ＋ on the dashboard) and Kaizen's AI
organizes it into a course with topics, grade categories, and a full semester of
assignments — several already graded, so **Grades, GPA, and Mastery light up
immediately** instead of an empty dashboard.

Everything below is original text written for the demo. It references real,
openly-licensed textbooks *nominatively* only:
- **OpenStax, _Algebra and Trigonometry 2e_** (Rice University, CC BY 4.0)
- **OpenStax, _Biology 2e_** (Rice University, CC BY 4.0)

No copyrighted passages are reproduced. "AP" refers to the College Board's
Advanced Placement program; Kaizen is not endorsed by or affiliated with the
College Board (trademark used nominatively — see the AP® note in the audit).

Dates assume a Fall 2026 term and a student pasting this in **late October 2026**
(just after the midterm), so there's a real graded history plus upcoming work.

## Demoing the whole flow

1. Paste **Block A**, review what Kaizen extracted, build the dashboard.
2. Open **Grades** — a real GPA and per-category breakdown appear from the graded
   items; the rational-expressions quiz visibly pulls the average down.
3. Tap that weak topic → **Study** in *Teach me* mode → each reply is a full
   mini-lesson with a worked example; grade the session and watch **Mastery** and
   the spaced-repetition schedule update.
4. Add **Block B** the same way to show multi-course GPA.
5. The per-course source materials (syllabus, textbook chapters, homework, and
   exams under `algebra-2/` and `ap-biology/`) are there if you want to upload a
   real file through intake instead of pasting.
