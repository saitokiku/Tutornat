## 1. Who can open an account

An account is opened by an adult: a parent or guardian, or an adult learning for themselves. A
learner under 18 uses the service only through a profile that the account holder creates and
controls. Teen profiles (13 to 17) get their own login name under the account. Profiles for learners
**under 13 — which includes the 9-to-12 band** — can be created but stay locked, and collect nothing
beyond the name and birth year you type, until the parental-consent flow described in the privacy
policy is live and you have completed it.

By opening an account you confirm you are 18 or over and that you are the parent or legal guardian of
any learner under 18 you add.

## 2. What the service is

Natural Tutor is a one-to-one AI tutor. It works by voice and text, draws on a shared whiteboard,
asks check questions, keeps a record of each session, and shows the account holder a per-skill
progress report.

The tutor is software. It is presented as a stylised character, it is labelled "AI tutor" throughout
every session, and it says so when asked. It is not a person, not a teacher of record, not a
substitute for school, and not a diagnostic or clinical service of any kind.

## 3. Coach mode

For a problem that looks like graded work, the tutor withholds the final answer until the learner has
attempted at least one step, then offers a worked solution and a similar problem to try. You agree
not to present the tutor's output as the learner's own work where a school or examination body
prohibits it.

## 4. Accuracy, and the limits of what the report means

The tutor can be wrong. Explanations, grading and progress estimates are produced by a language model
and are estimates.

**The parent report is generated by software. It is not a professional assessment.** Nothing in it is
an evaluation by a teacher, a psychologist, an educational diagnostician or any other qualified
person. A per-skill number is an estimate from a handful of check items, and the product labels it as
an estimate everywhere (`lib/tutor/report/parent-report.ts` renders "on track (estimate)" and "in
progress (estimate)" and reserves the word *mastery* for a confirmed, unaided check). Do not rely on
it for placement, for a diagnosis, for an IEP or 504 decision, or for any determination about a
child's ability or needs.

Use the report button in any session to flag an answer that is wrong or inappropriate. A flagged
session creates a record that a person reviews.

## 5. Plan, trial and payment

One plan: `[[$29]]` a month for up to `[[3]]` learner profiles and `[[8]]` hours of tutoring a month,
pooled across the profiles on the account. Learners aged 13 and over and adults get a `[[30]]`-minute
free trial with no card.

Under-13 profiles have no free trial: the parent pays first, and the card transaction is intended to
double as the identity check for parental consent, with a `[[7]]`-day money-back guarantee.

> **Draft note.** That consent mechanism is not built (see `open-questions.md` Q6), and counsel must
> confirm that a real charge is required — the minors-privacy skill records the working assumption

> description of the product.

Minutes are metered per session. A session stops when the minutes on the account run out, and the
dashboard warns at `[[80]]` percent. Payments are processed by Stripe under its own terms; we never
see your full card number.

## 6. Cancellation and refunds

Cancel at any time from the billing page, through the customer portal, in one step. No retention
flow, no interstitial, no phone call. The subscription ends at the close of the paid period and is
not renewed. Apart from the under-13 money-back guarantee above, fees for a period already begun are
not refunded unless the law where you live requires it.

## 7. Acceptable use

- Do not use the service to obtain sexual, violent or hateful content, or to make the tutor act as a
  person or a romantic partner.
- Do not enter another person's personal information into a session.
- Do not share an account or a teen login outside the family on the account.
- Do not probe, overload, or attempt to circumvent the service's safety rules.

We may suspend an account that breaks these rules. If a learner discloses self-harm or abuse, the
tutor responds with care, provides crisis resources, stops tutoring, and the session is flagged to
the account holder and for review.

## 8. Your content, and our software

Problems you upload, what the learner says, and what the tutor produces in response stay yours to
export and delete. You grant us the licence needed to run the service: to transcribe, store and
process that content for tutoring, for the progress report, and for safety review. We do not use it
to train AI models.

The software, the character, the voices and the skill content are ours or our licensors'. The engine
underneath is built on OpenMAIC, released under the MIT licence; its notice is kept in the repository
and in the site footer.

## 9. Termination and deletion

You can delete a learner profile or the whole account from the data page. Filing the request freezes
the profile immediately; erasure follows on the schedule in the retention policy. We may close
accounts that breach these terms, with notice where the law requires it.

## 10. Liability

The service is provided as is. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for indirect losses,
and our total liability for any claim is limited to the fees you paid in the three months before the
claim arose. Nothing here limits rights that consumer law gives you and that cannot be waived.
