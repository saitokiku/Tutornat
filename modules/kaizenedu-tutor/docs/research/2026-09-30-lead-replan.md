## 1. Diagnosis

The product is a carefully guarded text tutor with voice attached as batch input and batch output, and it fails the "could replace a human" test on all three things a human tutor actually provides. First, presence: a human answers within a second and makes a sound while thinking; this pipeline spends 600 ms of enforced silence, uploads a whole WAV, runs batch ASR, pays roughly fourteen database round trips across three serverless functions, waits ~775 ms for the first sentence (the only measured hop), then waits for a whole TTS clip to synthesize and decode before a syllable plays. Read from code that is 2.3-3.1 s p50 from end of speech on wifi and worse on LTE, against a 1.5 s budget nobody has measured (docs/SPIKE-latency.md:3 says so; the only real harness run failed every turn on a Forbidden TTS key). While it waits the learner sees a dashed ring and the word "Thinking", not what was heard. Second, knowing the learner: outside twelve fraction skills there is no graph, no prerequisites, no diagnostic and one coarse estimate per subject; sessions open from a nine-chip subject form written for a parent, then a second screen, then a model-generated greeting allowed 10 s; there is no session goal, no plan, and the re-teach memory stores the literal string "re-teach requested". Third, being right: the 118-item bank has zero reviewed items so every check including the placement diagnostic is invented live by the fast model with thinking off, and nothing verifies the tutor's arithmetic before it is spoken. Underneath all of that the key is a free-tier Gemini key at 5 requests a minute whose terms reportedly forbid an under-18 audience, and the cost ledger is charging a 6x over-estimate that will cut a 25-minute teen session off and stop a guest after a few sessions. The mastery law, coach mode, safety screen and sycophancy rules are genuinely good and ahead of the market; they are wrapped in a product that is slow, silent, blind to the learner and unmeasured.

## 3. Latency architecture decision

Decision: Upgrade A, the streaming cascade. Keep the text brain (tag grammar, server-side grading, pre-model safety screen, per-turn ceiling, evals all survive unchanged) and stream both edges. Speech-to-speech (Upgrade B) is not started until A2 is measured on a phone; the reports agree it breaks the tag grammar, the ceiling check, the pre-model safety screen, the Vercel hosting model, and (on Gemini) the under-18 terms. It becomes an option only for the 13+ bands if A2 lands above ~1.8 s p50 on a phone.

## 5. Stop doing

- Stop writing latency work without a number. No PR on the turn path merges without a same-day `pnpm latency --product` before/after and a phone `?metrics=1` figure pasted in. The spike doc's own rule ("no number here is an estimate") governs.
- Stop the two-press start and the "Ready when you are" screen. The gesture that presses the button is the gesture that unlocks audio.
- Stop leading with the level and subject form. It becomes the grown-ups' disclosure. Retire the "Before kindergarten" and K-3 guest levels from the public front door until there is a supervised mode; Common Sense Media says keep voice AI away from those ages.
- Stop running under-18 bands through the Gemini API on a free-tier key. Route them today with the variable that exists; route back only after a person reads the primary terms or Vertex is adopted.
- Stop running grading, diagnosis, summary and model-update on a reasoning model at three times the price and on the same 5 RPM quota as the live turn.
- Stop presenting model-authored checks and diagnostic placement as reviewed. Flag it until the bank is stamped.
- Stop the pebble as a face project. No further avatar or Rive work until the name plus level-driven ring is in and five kids have seen it.
- Stop evaluating speech-to-speech, ElevenLabs Agents, Deepgram Voice Agent, Hume or Nova until A1 and A2 are measured. They move the record, the ceilings and the safety screen off our server for a gain that may not exist once the edges stream.
- Stop the five-empty-panel Learn page for guests.
- Stop documenting unbuilt behaviour as live (README:79 on the per-band pause; the "Design table" in the voice-pipeline skill describes a streaming route that does not exist).
- Stop treating invariants as tested when the route-level trips are not counted: `latency.test.ts` caps media routes at 4 and the engine at 3 but nothing counts principal and rate-limit hops.
