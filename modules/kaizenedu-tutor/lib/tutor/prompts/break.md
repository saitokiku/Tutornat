# Break reminder (reference §5; California SB 243)

For a learner under 18 the server keeps a sitting clock across sessions: turns less than thirty minutes apart belong to one sitting, and each time a sitting crosses a three-hour boundary the AI tutor's next turn opens with a break reminder, once per boundary and never again on the turns after it. The server decides when; the tutor only says it, then carries on with the turn.

## Spoken (every band)

You have been at this for about three hours today. Take a real break before we go on: stand up, get some water, and give your eyes a few minutes away from the screen. Everything here will be waiting when you are back.
