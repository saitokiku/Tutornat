# UX critique — foundation build, 2026-10-07

Method: scripted walkthrough (Playwright, real Chrome) at 1440×900 and 390×844 as one family —
Maria (parent, Spanish is her first language, phone), Leo (kindergarten, can't read yet), Sofía
(grade 6, Spanish) — then each screen read in character for Leo, a 9-year-old, a 14-year-old with
homework due, Maria, and a 71-year-old grandparent. Mechanical checks: no horizontal overflow at any
size, no console errors, small-target scan. Then the pragmatism filter (RED fix now · GREEN good idea ·
YELLOW later · WHITE noise).

## In character

**Leo, 5.** "I can't read the box. I pressed the big black one and it went to words. The words had
buttons that said things. Mom had to read me every one. The plant one was fun when mom read it."

**Jayden, 9.** "I typed dinosaurs and it made a course and then every lesson said not written yet.
Why did it let me make it then?"

**Ava, 14.** "Homework on slope is due tomorrow. The ready-made one is actually fine. The made-up one
is an empty outline — useless tonight."

**Maria, parent.** "I made my account, I added my kids, okay. Then Leo pressed 'Parent' and was in my
page. He could have deleted Sofía. And it says '1 lessons'. And for Sofía it shows a course in English
with no warning."

**Walt, 71.** "Settings and Sign out are tiny gray words at the bottom. The back links are small."

## Filtered

| # | Finding | Class | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pre-readers can't hear individual sentences, choices or sorter items; Read aloud skips the items they must act on | RED | Tap-to-hear on every learner-facing text in the stage (on by default for K–2), and Read aloud includes widget items |
| 2 | A child can open Parent mode, manage learners, and reach Settings → delete all data | RED | Grown-up gate (multiplication question) for Parent, manage and add; learner Settings shows only learner things |
| 3 | K–2 Home leads with a text box; nothing to tap without reading | RED | For K–2, lead with big picture tiles of ready-made courses; magic box below, labelled for a grown-up |
| 4 | No pictures for courses anywhere | RED | Course art drawn from each course's own visuals/widgets, used in tiles, rows and catalogue |
| 5 | Template lessons dead-end ("not written yet") with nothing to do | RED | Course page and empty lesson offer the closest ready-made course; outline step says so before creating |
| 6 | "1 lessons", "0 · 0 · 0" sentences | RED | Plural-aware counts; Family shows figures, and "No activity this week" when empty; finish screen hides an all-zero line |
| 7 | Sign out from inside the app lands on sign-in, not the landing page | RED | Hard navigation after sign-out |
| 8 | Spanish learner is offered an English course with no label | RED | "In English" tag wherever course language ≠ learner language |
| 9 | Tutor panel (not connected) takes a quarter of the stage | RED | Collapsed by default to a slim toggle; stage gets the width |
| 10 | Small text targets: rail footer links, back links, auth footer links | RED | ≥ 40px targets |
| 11 | Magic box noise: "0 / 2000", "⌘ Enter" for kids and phones | GREEN | Counter only near the limit; shortcut hint only for grade 3+ on desktop |
| 12 | Subject guess: "dinosaurs" → Other | GREEN | Wider science keywords |
| 13 | K–2 stage text too small; scene kinds are tiny mono words | GREEN | Larger stage type for K–2; icons for Look/Try/Check/Do |
| 14 | Phone stage shows "Scene 3 of 6" twice | GREEN | Scenes toggle shows the scene title instead |
| 15 | Family assign dropdown starts on "—" | GREEN | "Choose a course" |
| 16 | Story sorter items have no pictures | YELLOW | Needs an illustration set; tap-to-hear covers it for now |
| 17 | Browser voices vary in quality | YELLOW | Real TTS comes with the tutor phase |
| 18 | "Make it look like a game" (Jayden) | WHITE | Not the product; stays calm |
