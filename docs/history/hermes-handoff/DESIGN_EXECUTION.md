## Locked owner direction

- US families; English and Spanish from the first version.
- Kindergarten through grade 8. K–2, grades 3–5 and grades 6–8 are provisional experience bands within K–8; never show high-school or preschool as supported.
- Parents are the customer and active assisted participants. The student gets a standalone natural AI companion, academic organization, learning support, accountability and a learning ledger.
- Human tutors are a later add-on: no tutor booking, tutor workspace, marketplace, dispatch or tutor prerequisite in this prototype.
- Schoolwork companion is primary: assignments, timelines, tests, teacher feedback and follow-through. Owner also wants curriculum/structured-learning capability; complete curriculum coverage is not verified and must not be claimed as an automatic by-product.
- Deep math and literacy support first; organize other subjects without implying equal instructional/assessment coverage.
- Parent progress, work status, help-needed flags and relevant learning detail; age-appropriate clearly disclosed access. Parents contribute feedback, not just inspect a dashboard. Observations are evidence with provenance, not automatically verified mastery.
- Voice plus shared visual workspace is central; complete typed/non-voice path is also required.
- Automate academic administration and draft plans, not the student's graded thinking, external submissions, teacher messages, or changes to real accounts without approval.
