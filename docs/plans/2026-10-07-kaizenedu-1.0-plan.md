# KaizenEDU 1.0 — the plan for the final K–9 product

2026-10-07. Supersedes [2026-10-07-real-product-plan.md](2026-10-07-real-product-plan.md) (kept for the
gap review). Owner's brief, in order, today: the dashboard · the ultimate tutor · the shadow worker · a
visual dashboard that makes academic life easy · learning any custom topic creates a course · the magic
box sorts homework, assignments and coursework · a chatbot to talk about topics and tutor · calendar and
course organization so a learner sees their path · deterministic knowledge from real sources · more
interactive · "one record and one mechanism underneath, many faces on top" · K–9 first · "be ready for a
first production build to impress me, but the philosophy should be what you know."

## 0. The philosophy, restated once

1. **Show it, let them touch it, then check it.** Text is the backup channel.
2. **Code checks answers; AI talks.** Nothing a model says becomes a fact in the record.
3. **Practice is not proof.** On your own · with help · not yet · proved later, on different days.
4. **One next step on every screen.** The shadow work is ours, not the family's.
5. **Parents are partners with real information** — never scores, never surveillance.
6. **Real sources, named and linked.** We never copy, never invent a citation.
7. **No engagement tricks.** No streaks to lose, points, leaderboards or "come back" nudges to children.
8. **No AI slop.** Plain, warm, specific. Everything AI-made says so.
9. **Safety before any model.** A crisis gets a person's words, not a model's, and the family is told.
10. **Works without AI.** AI makes it better; it is never the floor.
11. **Natural to use.** A child stays because the work is worth doing, not because anything makes them
    stay; a parent is present with minimal expectations — set up once, glance weekly, act only when
    something matters (§2.10).

## 1. Positioning — three doors, one engine

The front of the product asks three questions, and each is a door into the same thing:

| Door | The ask | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Stuck on homework right now?** | photo or text into the magic box | the tutor works it through with them — asks, hints, shows a similar problem worked out; never does it for them |
| **A test coming?** | "I have a test on fractions Friday" | it lands on the calendar; prep sets land on the plan for the three days before; the tutor and the parent can see which skills are proved and which aren't |
| **Need to keep up in general?** | setup once | the daily plan, the path and the record do the shadow work; the parent glances at a weekly email |

The reason to believe, in the owner's words: "we aren't just a Claude and rich text." It is
code-checked practice + real, cited knowledge + an AI tutor with tools + a visual stage + voice, all
reading and writing **one record**, connected so the learner never re-explains anything and feels
frictionless — **growth mode**, kept on by the shadow worker rather than by the family's willpower.

Copy rule (claims discipline): the landing promises only what a visitor can do that day. "Narrated,
touchable lessons" and "a tutor that talks" go up when the voice vendor is live; "video" only when
real video exists (1.1).

## 1.1 One record, one engine, many faces

```
                   Today · Practice · Talk · Learn · Calendar · Family · Growth · Records
                          (faces — each shows a slice of the same record)
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Planner      Mastery law     Intake         Course builder     Tutor        Knowledge
   (what next)  (what counts)   (what is it)   (make a course)    (talk+tools) (real facts)
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                      THE RECORD — accounts · learners · attempts (append-only) · sets ·
                      school items · classes · teacher notes · school results · plan marks ·
                      reading · tutor threads · courses · notes        (Postgres, synced)
```

Everything a face shows is computed from the record by pure functions (`learning/`, `planner/`,
`practice/`). Everything a face changes goes through `lib/*` actions. That is why one record can
serve a kindergartner, a 7th grader, a homeschool parent and a parent at 8 pm with a worksheet.

## 2. The product, face by face (the 1.0 state)

Each face lists: what you see · what you can do · what is behind it · K–2 difference.

### 2.1 Today — the dashboard

- **See:** greeting and date; a status strip (due today · test in N days · checks ready · minutes
  left in today's budget); the one raised **Next** card; the rest of today's plan as rows with done
  marks; **Coming up at school** (7 days); **Courses in progress** (continue points); the **magic box**.
- **Do:** start the next thing in one tap; mark school work done; open Get help (tutor with the item);
  "I have a test coming" (calendar add, prefilled); ask the magic box for anything.
- **Behind:** `planFor()` (checks → prep → due → teacher notes → daily sets → reviews → lesson),
  `comingUp()`, `continueTarget()`, goals from setup decide what leads.
- **K–2:** picture-first tiles, read-aloud on every line, no clock, the tutor speaks first.

### 2.2 The magic box — universal intake (new)

- **See:** one box: "What's going on? Learn something, homework, a test, or practice." Chips for
  common asks. A classifier shows its guess as a small choice row before anything happens.
- **Do:** type or paste; drop a photo/PDF/worksheet; pick what it is if the guess is wrong.
- **Behind** (`lib/intake.ts`): rules first (dates → school item; "test/quiz/exam" → test;
  "homework/worksheet/due/page/pp." → homework; "practice/drill/more of" → practice; "learn/why/how/
  what is" or anything else → learn), AI extraction when connected (`/api/ai/extract`), skill matching
  (`planner/skillmatch.ts`) with the subject from the learner's classes.
  - **Homework / assignment / coursework** → a school item (date, class, linked skills, the pasted text
    or photo as attachment) → the **item page** with Get help, a prep set, resources, mark done.
  - **Test / quiz** → a school item; prep sets appear on the plan for the 3 days before; the item page
    shows which linked skills are proved / practicing / not started.
  - **Practice X** → a set on the matching skill, or AI questions for an open topic (labelled).
  - **Learn X** → the course builder (2.5).
- **K–2:** the grown-up uses it; the child's version is picture tiles.

### 2.3 Practice — the at-home Kumon

- **See:** subject tabs; checks ready; the one **Up next** skill with status and sources; "Practice
  anything" search (skills first, then AI questions); "Find my level"; the full **skill map** by grade
  with status dots (new · practicing · check opens · passed 1 of 2 · proved · needs a refresh) and the
  standard's actual wording on tap (Common Core text from the knowledge layer).
- **Do:** start a set; start a check; run placement; practice any skill; see what a standard means.
- **The set:** one problem at a time; keypad / fraction pad / remainder / choices / text / expression;
  **tap-to-mark counting**, **number-line tap**, **fraction-bar tap**, **clock set**, **balance scale**
  inputs where the skill calls for it (new); hint ladder (nudge → strategy → first step); worked steps
  after two tries; the tutor beside the problem; skip and fix at the end; honest finish with pace.
- **Behind:** `practice/` generators (seeded, EN/ES), `practice/answer.ts` checker, `learning/engine.ts`
  (level stepping, status, reviews, placement), `lib/practice.ts`.
- **K–2:** 6 problems, pictures on every item, choices or tap inputs only, no timer, everything spoken.

### 2.4 Talk — the tutor (the chatbot that tutors)

- **See:** a full-screen conversation with a **board** on the right (desktop) or above (phone) where
  pictures, worked examples, definitions, cited facts, practice and calendar cards appear. The same
  tutor sits in a drawer beside every problem and on the lesson stage.
- **Do:** type, talk (push-to-talk, grown-up permission), send a photo of the problem; tap quick asks
  ("give me a hint", "show a similar one", "explain it a different way", "what does … mean", "find me a
  book about …"); start the practice or add the date the tutor offers.
- **Behind:**
  - **With AI** (`lib/ai/tutor.ts`): Socratic rules by grade band, tools `next_hint`, `check_answer`,
    `similar_problem`, `find_skill`, `find_resources`, `show_visual`, `start_practice`,
    `add_to_calendar`, `note_for_grownup` + **new** `look_up` (Wikipedia summary, cited), `define_word`,
    `find_book`, `read_poem`, `standard_text`. No answer key in the prompt. Spend cap per learner.
  - **Without AI** (demo tutor, `lib/tutor-demo.ts`): still useful — for a problem: vetted hints, a
    worked similar problem, the first step; for a topic: the Wikipedia extract with its link, the
    definition, the matching catalogue lesson's key points, the practice that fits, and sources. It
    says it is the demo tutor in one line, once.
  - **Safety screen** before any model, fixed referrals, a note for the family, transcripts on the
    child's page, break reminder at 3 hours.
- **K–2:** the tutor speaks first; replies read aloud by sentence; big tap chips; no typing needed.

### 2.5 Learn — courses and the path

- **See:** the magic box ("Learn anything") at the top; then **per subject**: your courses in order
  (progress, next lesson, "from a grown-up" first), the **suggested next course** for the grade, and a
  one-line skills summary (proved / practicing / total). Below, the full ready-made catalogue by band.
- **Do:** continue; add a ready-made course; build a new one; reorder your path (move a course up/down).
- **A course** has lessons; **a lesson** is a stage of scenes: slide (with a real picture), interactive
  (a widget), quiz (hint + explanation), project (at home). Three origins, always labelled:
  1. **Written by people** (catalogue; teacher-reviewed).
  2. **Built from real sources** (new, works without AI): overview from Wikipedia (cited extract +
     link), key terms with definitions, the closest catalogue lesson's scenes, a practice interactive
     from the skill map, "find out more" with sources and books. Deterministic; labelled "Built from
     Wikipedia and real sources".
  3. **Written by AI** (with a provider): passes the gates or is left out and named; cached by
     (goal, grade, language) so the next family gets it instantly.
- **Behind:** `lib/courses.ts`, `lib/generate.ts`, `lib/ai/build.ts`, `knowledge/`, `catalogue/`.

### 2.6 The lesson stage

- **See:** scene list, the scene, tutor panel; **narrated slides** with word highlights (new; the
  tutor's voice when a voice vendor is set, the browser's otherwise); Look / Try / Check / Do icons.
- **Do:** tap and keyboard everything; read aloud; ask the tutor about this scene; finish → honest
  tally and sources.
- **Widgets (1.0 set):** fraction bar, number line, states of matter, moon phases, sorter — plus
  **area model**, **place-value blocks**, **clock**, **balance scale (equations)**, **coordinate
  plotter**, **food web / sequence builder**, **sentence builder**, **word sort**. Each: tap + keyboard,
  text equivalent, reduced-motion path.
- **Later (1.1):** AI-generated interactives in a sandboxed frame (OpenMAIC's host), whiteboard
  drawing, playback as a short video.

### 2.7 Calendar & School — the shadow worker's desk

- **See:** a **week** (desktop) / **day list** (phone) where each day shows its school items *and* its
  plan (done marks for past days, projected sets for coming days); prep days and check openings;
  classes; teacher notes; scores from school.
- **Do:** add/edit items; import (paste text, .ics file, class calendar link, photo with AI); open an
  **item page** (new): notes and attached text/photo, linked skills with status, Get help, prep set,
  resources, mark done; refresh a linked class calendar; export .ics.
- **Behind:** `planner/{plan,ics,intake,skillmatch}.ts`, `/api/ics` (SSRF-guarded), `lib/school.ts`.

### 2.8 Family — the parent's view

- **See:** one card per child: today's status and next step, the week in honest numbers, **nudges**
  (a check waiting 14 days, a stuck skill, a test in 3 days with no prep, nothing done in 5 days),
  and a link to the child's page.
- **Child page:** today, the week, proved / checks waiting / stuck / help needed, skill maps, school
  coming up, notes (tutor and safety notes marked), every tutor conversation, reading log with free
  libraries, settings (minutes, subjects, timer, microphone permission, interests, starting points),
  the optional AI note written only from the numbers.
- **Records:** a dated log by subject with CSV and print (homeschool). Records; doesn't certify.
- **Weekly email** (new, opt-in) from the same numbers; nothing for an empty week; never to children.

### 2.9 Setup, settings, landing

- **Setup:** account → "What should KaizenEDU help with?" (help now · daily practice · homeschool ·
  staying organized) → learners (name, grade, language) → optional placement per subject.
- **Settings:** language, goals, AI status, learners, data export/delete, about.
- **Landing:** the four jobs, how it works, ready-made courses, parents, subjects, honest status.

### 2.10 Natural to use — how engagement works without tricks

We don't use streaks, points, badges or notifications to children. What keeps a child in the chair:

| Rule | In the product |
|---|---|
| **It starts easy.** The first sets feel doable (Kumon's comfortable start: one skill below level). | placement starts a grade below; level stepping; "not yet", never "wrong" |
| **It's short and it ends.** A sitting is one set or one lesson; the finish is a clean stop, not a hook for the next. | 6/10 problems; K–2 budget 10 min; "I'm done for today" is always honoured; the next thing is offered, never auto-started |
| **The child is in charge of the order.** Structure decides *what*, the child decides *which first*. | Today's plan is a list they can start anywhere; the box takes any topic |
| **Something to touch on every screen.** | tap inputs, widgets, pictures first, read-aloud |
| **Curiosity is a door, not a detour.** "Why does the Moon change?" becomes a course in a minute. | magic box → source-built or AI course; the tutor answers with real, cited facts |
| **Progress you can see and believe.** | the path (Learn), proved skills, honest numbers — no invented scores |
| **A voice that talks first for little ones.** | K–2: the tutor speaks first; replies spoken; big chips |
| **Nothing nags.** | no reminders to children; the plan simply still shows the next thing tomorrow |

What a parent has to do: set up once (5 minutes), answer the "what should we help with" question,
glance at the weekly email, and act on a nudge when one arrives (a check waiting, a stuck skill, a
test with no prep). Everything else — filing school dates, choosing today's work, deciding when a
check is due, finding sources — is the shadow worker's job. Target: **under 5 minutes a week of parent
time unless they choose more**, measured in the pilot alongside "returns without prompting".

### 3.1 Deterministic knowledge — what works, what doesn't (probed 2026-10-07)

| Source | Use | Status | Licence / note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wikipedia REST summary (en, es) | "What is X" with citation; course overviews | ✓ built | CC BY-SA; show extract + link, attribute |
| Datamuse | definitions (Wiktionary), rhymes, syllables, related words | ✓ built | free, no key, English |
| Open Library | book search, covers, borrow links; reading-log autocomplete | ✓ built | open data |
| LibriVox | public-domain audiobooks | ✓ built | public domain |
| Wikisource (en, es) | public-domain texts | ✓ built | CC BY-SA / PD |
| PoetryDB | public-domain poems, full text | ✓ built | PD |
| Common Standards Project | Common Core standard wording by code | ✓ built | open data |
| Open-Meteo, USGS earthquakes | live data for science word problems | ✓ reachable | free; 1.1 |
| OpenStax CMS API | book list/metadata; deep links into free textbooks | ✓ reachable | CC BY; link, don't ingest in 1.0 |
| Oak National Academy API | reviewed lessons + quizzes (UK curriculum) | needs free token | OGL v3, commercial OK with attribution; 1.1 candidate for reviewed questions |
| Gutendex (Gutenberg API) | book catalogue | down (522) during probe | retry later; Open Library covers it |
| Wikidata | facts/entities | ✓ reachable | 1.1 |
| LibreTexts, CK-12, Khan | — | no public API / CK-12 forbids for-profit use | link only |
| NASA APOD | — | errored | not needed |

Rule for all of it: fetched server-side, cached a day, inputs capped, no learner identifiers forwarded,
every fact shown with its source and link, never paraphrased as our own.

## 6. Quality bars (what "good" means, measurably)

- **Correctness:** every computed skill verified by an independent route over ≥ 200 seeds per level,
  both languages; mutation checks on every strand; no draft bank ships as reviewed without a reviewer.
- **Tutor:** eval set of 40 scripted conversations; ≥ 90% deterministic pass (no answer before a try,
  ≤ 2 sentences + 1 question, no praise words, tools used for arithmetic, right language); median
  latency to first token < 1.5 s; cost per turn logged.
- **Voice:** first audio < 1.5 s after a reply starts; barge-in stops speech < 300 ms; a child's "mhm"
  does not end the tutor's sentence.
- **Honesty:** no number on any parent surface comes from a model; every AI-made thing is labelled;
  every fact has a source link (tests assert the label and the link).
- **Accessibility:** axe serious/critical = 0 on every screen at 1440 and 390; keyboard parity; 44 px
  targets (56 px for K–2 primary); reduced-motion equivalents; 320 px without overflow.
- **Performance:** Today interactive < 2 s on a mid phone; a practice set opens < 1 s; lesson stage
  first scene < 2 s.
- **Privacy:** no third-party scripts; knowledge calls carry only the query; names never leave the
  device except to our own server.
- **Tests:** unit on every pure module; e2e journeys for the four jobs at both sizes; evals with AI.
- **Natural use (pilot):** ≥ 60% of learners return without being told to; parents spend < 5 minutes a
  week in the app unless they choose more; no child-facing reminder exists anywhere in the code.
